Bug fixes:
- MeasuredOverhang measured the wrong glyphs after composed characters or ligatures
- StyleManager could lose an inherited font width
- HitTestLine threw if called before layout
- Buffers reallocated on every grow instead of doubling
- ResetPooledMemory stopped pooled objects being reset

Performance:
- Much less memory allocated during layout and paint (paint now allocates nothing)
- Fonts used for font fallback are now disposed
